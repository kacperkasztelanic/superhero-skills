# From choice to art direction

Read after stage 6. Develop only the chosen leading direction and, optionally, one supporting direction. Each is a decision mechanism, not a palette, font, or layout preset.

## Shared mechanism

Write one sentence: **[material] conveys [meaning for this offer] through [composition rule], developed across [sequence], with [constraint maintaining coherence].**

For example, photographs of hands and ceramic details reveal craft through alternating full views and close crops, moving from silhouette to use, with quiet backgrounds and text clear of important details. This describes a mechanism, not a universal product recipe.

Resolve:

- What dominates the opening, why, and what information appears before scrolling?
- How do scale, density, and empty space change later? What connects compositions?
- Which material provides evidence and which atmosphere? Can visitors distinguish them?
- How does text relate to material: beside it, as a caption, within the scene, on a separate plane?
- What remains distinctive without color/animation? What must change on a phone?

This tests composition, not permission to remove agreed dynamic motion. Complete the mandatory [artistic synthesis](artistic-synthesis.md). Develop the opening, key later scene, and finale as variations of the same principle.

## Dynamic development of the chosen material

These are possibilities for rhythmic or stronger motion, not a checklist. Choose an appropriate mechanism and design its states, relationships, and continuity. For static/quiet versions preserve equivalent compositional relationships.

| Direction | Possible mechanism across scenes |
| --- | --- |
| Typography | Word arrangement, scale, or a variable axis reveals meaning; a word/line becomes a frame or transition. |
| Photography | Wide framing moves into a significant detail; image/caption layers lead attention and reframing connects scenes. |
| Film | Shots and text rhythm develop one transformation; poster/static versions preserve the argument, not just a background. |
| Physical product | Silhouette becomes an enlarged detail or exploded layers explaining function; object continuity connects stages. |
| Software demo | Visitor input starts a visible process; its result becomes the next scene's dominant, explaining value. |
| Illustrated world | Characters, background, traces, and symbols occupy separate planes; choreography/parallax reveals a relationship or change. |
| Diagrams | Relationships assemble to explain a mechanism, then a chosen path leads to an outcome or use scene. |
| Data | A new question/scenario reorganizes the same data to reveal a relationship; axes/labels preserve interpretation. |
| Gallery/editorial | Changes in scale, image rhythm, and related captions lead from a collection to a work and its meaning. |
| Space/3D | Camera movement, a section, or configuration reveals a property; the same object anchors the next argument. Static renders provide fallback. |
| Collage | Layers have distinct roles/speeds, masks open relationships, part of a scene becomes the next scene's axis; text participates. |

Do not copy examples as finished direction. Name the actual elements, change, meaning, and mobile behavior. Section reveals and image switches may support dynamic work but cannot replace its agreed motion system.

## 1. Typography

- **Material/meaning:** letterforms establish brand voice, hierarchy, tension, and reading pace.
- **Composition:** define display/body scale, text width, and line breaks; develop contrasts of scale and space across scenes. Not every paragraph becomes a monumental heading.
- **Parameters:** actually available licensed fonts with needed characters, weight roles, optical alignment, leading, tracking, and line length. Expressive headings preserve information readability.
- **Behavior:** motion emphasizes the meaning of specific text; do not animate every letter for effect alone.
- **Mobile/fallback:** intentional new breaks, fluid scale, legible text, and no wait to access content. Reduced motion preserves hierarchy.

## 2. Editorial photography

- **Material/meaning:** specific shots show atmosphere, people, use, or materiality.
- **Composition:** whole -> detail -> context; wide frames against narrower text, related captions, defined focal points.
- **Parameters:** consistent light, temperature, treatment, proportions, and subject; direct separate mobile images if a wide crop loses meaning.
- **Behavior:** displacement, reveal, or reframing should lead to information. Parallax alone does not create narrative.
- **Mobile/fallback:** reserve dimensions, responsive sources, readable captions; the promise survives missing imagery. Do not fabricate reportage about a real company.

## 3. Film / cinematic storytelling

- **Material/meaning:** a sequence shows transformation, ritual, motion, or a place's character better than a single frame.
- **Composition:** relate film shots to text chapters; a full-screen hero video is not mandatory.
- **Parameters:** real source/file, format, loop duration, loading, poster, overlay contrast. Decorative film cannot depend on sound; essential speech requires captions/text.
- **Behavior:** visitors need not finish the film before understanding or acting. Provide appropriate stopping controls; justify costly scrubbing.
- **Mobile/fallback:** a poster/static sequence preserves meaning for reduced motion, errors, and autoplay restrictions. Do not substitute an unrelated abstract loop.

## 4. Physical product as protagonist

- **Material/meaning:** silhouette, texture, detail, and use explain the specific object's value.
- **Composition:** whole product -> benefit-supporting detail -> use context; callouts refer to visible properties.
- **Parameters:** object scale, surface, perspective, and lighting; photographs/renders faithfully represent what is sold.
- **Behavior:** view/variant controls change the correct imagery and description. Rotation should reveal meaningful properties.
- **Mobile/fallback:** readable detail, touch/keyboard controls, text labels for variants. Prefer good photography when 3D adds no value; preserve explicitly selected 3D requirements or report the unmet dependency.

## 5. Software demonstration

- **Material/meaning:** real input -> action -> output explains the benefit.
- **Composition:** a chosen flow and readable UI slice with brief context; output may dominate more than the dashboard.
- **Parameters:** demo scope, true states, simulation boundaries; labels/data match copy and control state.
- **Behavior:** one bounded interaction can reveal value without building an unsolicited app or pretending backend integration.
- **Mobile/fallback:** reorganize and simplify details rather than shrinking desktop into an illegible miniature; accessible explanation and sensible initial state.

## 6. Illustrated brand world

- **Material/meaning:** an original formal language turns abstract benefits into understandable situations/relationships.
- **Composition:** recurring motifs develop one metaphor and relate to specific statements/scenes.
- **Parameters:** geometry, perspective, outlines, texture, palette, and character/scene rules form a system; do not mix unrelated generated styles.
- **Behavior:** motion illustrates a process/relationship; intensity follows stage 10A.
- **Mobile/fallback:** dedicated crops/simplifications preserve meaning; images do not replace necessary instructions/labels or impersonate documentation of real events.

## 7. Diagrams / technical precision

- **Material/meaning:** nodes, stages, and relationships show the actual mechanism, architecture, or workflow.
- **Composition:** general model -> detail; labels/explanations are integral.
- **Parameters:** meaningful lines, directions, colors, and scale; no fictional integrations/data flows.
- **Behavior:** highlighting a relationship or selecting a stage provides explanation, not only a glowing line.
- **Mobile/fallback:** convert complexity into readable sequences/lists, without microscopic text or page-wide horizontal scrolling. Provide a text equivalent.

## 8. Data / information visualization

- **Material/meaning:** comparisons, distributions, or changes reveal an offer-relevant relationship.
- **Composition:** one question and takeaway per chart; descriptions interpret measures rather than decorate arbitrary numbers.
- **Parameters:** source, units, date range, scale, limitations. Label demo data, never portray it as customer results.
- **Behavior:** filters/scenarios update both chart and explanation; calculation assumptions are verifiable.
- **Mobile/fallback:** fewer marks, legible axes, accessible text/table. No conclusions dependent on hover or color alone.

## 9. Gallery / quiet editorial

- **Material/meaning:** selected work/objects provide the main argument through quality.
- **Composition:** sequence, scale, and pauses create rhythm; selection replaces decorative excess without mandating a regular grid.
- **Parameters:** work proportions, captions/authorship, density, and scale changes; contact/purchase stays obvious.
- **Behavior:** transitions support viewing. A lightbox needs keyboard access, dismissal, and focus management.
- **Mobile/fallback:** intentional ordering and sizes; do not bury most work behind difficult carousel controls.

## 10. Spatial form / 3D

- **Material/meaning:** space reveals shape, layers, relationships, or configuration changes.
- **Composition:** define viewpoint, scale, and relation to copy; space must not obscure text/actions.
- **Parameters:** available model, geometry, materials, lighting, loading/render budget, and real need for interactivity.
- **Behavior:** movement reveals information; do not require cursor tracking or perpetual rendering without a reason.
- **Mobile/fallback:** good renders/image sequences for absent WebGL, reduced motion, and slow devices. Do not add a heavy dependency only for an ornamental shape.

## 11. Graphic experiment / collage

- **Material/meaning:** shapes, contrasts, textures, and juxtapositions express the brand's specific idea.
- **Composition:** define overlap, grid, scale relationships, and recurring motif; later scenes develop the rule rather than add unrelated tricks.
- **Parameters:** a selected family of forms/behaviors, intentional layers, and text contrast. Ground motifs in the product, narrative, or concrete formal relationships; do not invent symbolism.
- **Behavior:** a coherent family of transformations connects the experience when motion is selected; avoid random bursts of effects.
- **Mobile/fallback:** redesign layers, framing, and order; adjust decorations that collide with content. Check readability in the actual composition.

## Hybrids and custom directions

Record leading/supporting materials and their roles, e.g. photography establishes atmosphere while diagrams explain the process. Resolve conflict in favor of understanding the offer and the chosen dominant. Develop custom directions through the same five fields: material/meaning, composition, parameters, behavior, mobile/fallback. Do not force them into the nearest preset.
