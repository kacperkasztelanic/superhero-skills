# Synthesis, execution prompt, and verification

Read after [artistic synthesis](artistic-synthesis.md), before coding. Concatenated option labels or precise CSS values do not constitute a deep prompt. Derive relationships: audience -> promise -> evidence -> visual thesis -> continuous composition -> choreography/action -> verification.

## LANDING_BRIEF.md - decision register

Record offer/scope, language, audience/situation, promise, primary CTA/endpoint, facts/sources, assets, brand/technical constraints, and delivery. Label hypotheses, proposals, and unavailable resources.

Decision table: stage, user answer, execution decision, rationale, status/provenance. Record last completed stage and first open decision. Full delegation produces agent proposals, not fabricated user answers.

Separately retain binding requirements and literal sources of key choices. Mark each selected evidence type as available, being sourced, honestly substituted, or an unmet dependency. An unattached asset is not necessarily nonexistent; local-only is not consent to a demo. "No open decisions" does not mean "all requirements realized".

Do not persist secrets, credentials, or unnecessary personal information from supplied screens. Reference resources, not tokens.

## DESIGN.md - project-specific specification

Support implementation and later assessment. Record:

- **Concept:** alternative interpretations and concise selection rationale; thesis connecting the offer to the visual idea; leading/supporting material and whole-page rule.
- **Opening:** promise copy, layout, dominant, visible CTA, text/material relationship; desktop/mobile plan.
- **Storyboard:** each scene's visitor question, answer/copy, evidence, arrangement/planes, asset, entry, development, exit, next-scene transition, CTA role. Explain continuity and scale/density changes, not just section names.
- **Typography:** actual available families/styles, source/license/limitations, language support, fallbacks, roles, scale, leading, line length, breaks. Choose values for the project.
- **Color:** binding palette/tokens, exact values, semantic roles, allowed tones, foreground/background pairs, interaction states; scene distribution and image/effect treatment. Preserve chosen families/values without arbitrary backgrounds/accents.
- **Geometry/rhythm:** widths, grids/alignment, spacing, proportions, density, scale/space changes; borders, radii, and textures only where intentionally selected.
- **Detail language:** selected caption, line, symbol, and accent families with starting point, concrete role, locations, and repetition scope. Distinguish brand material from agent proposals. Do not fill every category or rely on style labels; judge copy and individual uses in composition.
- **Assets:** files or sourcing plan, content, provenance, rights/attribution status, role, crop/focal point, mobile, dimensions, fallback. Separate decoration from evidence.
- **Motion score:** trigger/natural scroll range -> entry -> development/plane relationships -> meaningful point -> exit/continuity -> reversal -> mobile/reduced motion. Then technique, timing/easing, render cost. Distinguish scene motion, transitions, and responses. For static choices record equivalent spatial continuity/control states.
- **Signature interaction:** what the visitor does, what changes, what they learn; or intentional absence of a separate interaction.
- **Responsiveness/accessibility:** composition, order, line breaks, crops, navigation, interactions, semantics, and visual-content equivalents.
- **Technology/performance:** existing stack or justified simple choice, media/font loading, reserved dimensions, dependency/backend boundaries, CTA function.
- **Verification:** criteria and later actual viewport, interaction, correction, and limitation findings.
- **Compliance matrix:** requirement -> source/status -> implementation -> QA evidence -> result. Cover palette, material, composition, promised effects, and intensity separately.

For redesigns add baseline, retained constraints, and before -> intended -> purpose comparisons for opening and a key later scene. Preserve unrelated existing DESIGN.md content.

## LANDING_PROMPT.md - self-contained execution prompt

Write in the user's language unless requested otherwise. Fill with interview/synthesis specifics; no empty placeholders. Explicitly identify missing facts and honest omission/dependency handling. This is a full creative/execution commission, not a short interview report. Preserve the original Typography-First prompt's ambition for the selected direction.

Depth is not word count, effect count, or parameter count. Explain the whole experience, key transformations over time, and their reasons. Exact easing does not replace a score; naming collage does not create composition.

### 0. Creative mandate - preserve the original's strength

Open with a full mandate corresponding to these four paragraphs. Rewrite for the actual product, chosen material, character, and intensity rather than pasting empty labels:

> Design and build an original, visually exceptional, production-quality one-page landing page. Treat the chosen artistic material as the primary substance of space, hierarchy, rhythm, and atmosphere. Create one cohesive scrolling composition with distinctive identity, persuasive offer-specific copy, real assets, and a narrative from opening statement to final action. Do not settle for a conventional marketing layout with decorative pictures, interchangeable cards, placeholders, or disconnected tricks. Deliver a working responsive website.
>
> For the selected dynamic experience, make motion fundamental to composition and storytelling. Develop the principal material into a coherent language of choreography, depth/parallax, natural-scroll transitions, and responsive reactions. Use real optimized film or another intentionally constructed moving medium appropriate to this direction. Choose masking, clipping, layers, transforms, perspective, variable typography, compositing, or fluid transitions where they develop the concept. Design a sequence or interaction whose meaning a static frame cannot convey. Motion establishes pacing, reveals meaning, and connects the page while preserving readability, natural scrolling, and immediate access to action.
>
> Polish desktop and mobile as intentional versions of the same idea rather than shrinking the large composition onto a phone. Provide semantics, keyboard access, visible focus, contrast, and a coherent reduced-motion version. Optimize media, fonts, and animation, reserve space, maintain fluid scrolling, and use dependencies where they serve the project. Preserve the selected palette in imagery, media, and effects too.
>
> Run the complete page, inspect composition and sequences in motion on desktop/mobile, exercise every interaction, and fix errors, weak hierarchy, illegible material, broken continuity, palette drift, and jank. Do not stop at the first successful render: iterate until copy, material, compositions, transitions, and timing form one meticulously directed experience. Deliver implementation, run instructions, concept, motion system, and actual verification performed.

For a deliberately static/subtle choice adapt paragraph two to the user's limits: equally demanding direction of space/detail without mandatory film, parallax, or a separate showpiece. Keep quality, continuity, and iteration throughout. For dynamic choices do not reduce it to "add subtle section entrances".

After the mandate develop these blocks into concrete instructions:

### 1. Task and result

Build/redesign/polish a working ONE-PAGE LANDING PAGE for the specific offer. State language, scope, location/environment, preserved functions, and delivery. Do not smuggle publication or a backend beyond the user's choice.

### 2. Brief and argument

Who visits, their situation, what they must understand, promised outcome, and differentiation. Give evidence, sources, and limitations. Specify primary CTA copy, true destination, and next step. Identify brief-grounded objections without inventing customer research.

### 3. Art direction as a mechanism

State thesis, leading material/meaning, composition rules, supporting roles, stylistic boundaries. Describe opening, key later composition, finale, and their connection. Carry over hierarchy, palette/distribution, type, rhythm, crops, and detail. Default text-left/image-right requires a concept-grounded reason and development beyond it; style names without relationships are insufficient.

Transfer selected detail language: what it contributes and where it applies. Do not turn a local motif into required decoration for every section or add a decorative kit during implementation. Require assessment of visible effect and correction of weak instances while preserving compositional ambition. Do not copy a catalog of possible detail categories as a required list.

### 4. Narrative and copy

Transfer each scene's meaning, thought/copy, fact/evidence, dominant/planes, entry/development/exit, continuity motif, and action. Include finished promise/CTA copy and remaining copy outline. Describe scale/density rhythm beyond heading order. Write for this offer in its chosen voice; do not impose a component template or invent clients, testimonials, prices, integrations, or results.

### 5. Materials

List resources with files/URLs, provenance, status, purpose, and production needs. For new assets describe content, style, proportions, role, and palette. Plan separate layers, masks, crops, movement margins, and states. Include posters, responsive sources, and fallbacks where needed. Actually create and check required asset-prompt/provenance files. List environment-specific assets separately as attachments needed to reuse the prompt.

### 6. Motion and behavior

Describe the shared motion language, depth, plane relationships, and continuity. Carry over the full score: trigger/progress -> entry -> development -> meaning -> exit -> reversal -> variants. Distinguish scene motion, transitions, and microinteractions before technical means/parameters. Locate every agreed effect. Rhythmic and stronger cannot be realized entirely by fade-ins and tab crossfades. Preserve natural scrolling and immediate CTA access, touch/keyboard, and reduced motion. Sticky scenes with natural scrolling are allowed. Do not require every technique or a medium contrary to the direction.

### 7. Mobile, accessibility, execution

Give concrete small-screen composition/choreography adaptations preserving meaning/palette. Specify semantic structure, logical focus, visible states, contrast, labels, and equivalents. Establish stack, integration boundaries, validation, and true success/error states. Local execution permits needed fonts/libraries. Handle media dimensions, font loading, animation cost, and missing resources. Reduced motion preserves meaning without replacing the full version for everyone.

### 8. Procedure and completion criteria

Write DESIGN.md, build opening/transition/key later scene with real motion, inspect the sequence, then expand. Run and view the full desktop/mobile experience, exercise interactions, fix findings. Include specific criteria below and from the matrix. Finish with implementation location, run instructions, preview, and actual evidence. Do not claim unperformed tests or effects existing only in prose.

## Completeness before coding

- Can another agent implement from this prompt and its listed assets without guessing conversation decisions?
- Do promise/CTA fit audience and evidence?
- Does direction explain composition rather than name a style?
- Does the storyboard explain opening -> middle -> finale continuity rather than a standard page with added pictures?
- Does the prompt preserve the creative mandate and chosen direction/intensity's ambition?
- Do sequences specify states, plane relationships, meaning, transitions, and variants beyond effects/timing labels?
- Does every agreed requirement have implementation and verification rather than an easier substitution or "optional" label?
- Have concrete graphic rules filled the user's design-knowledge gaps?
- Do captions/lines/symbols have concrete roles/scope beyond an inherited kit or the agent's instruction to add them?
- Are materials, real function, and fallbacks available?
- Are desktop/mobile intentional, with a reduced-motion variant?
- Are local/hosting, prototype/connected function, and facts/hypotheses unambiguous?

Revise shallow concepts/prompts independently. Eight completed headings alone do not meet the target.

## Verify the implemented website

Use a supported browser tool. Code inspection/build cannot substitute for viewing the page. Without browser access disclose the limitation and do feasible checks. Do not install an unnecessary test stack for a static page.

**Correctness and usability:**

- View representative desktop (e.g. 1440x900) and phone (e.g. 390x844), suited to the content. Add a narrower screen such as 320px or a problematic breakpoint when justified.
- Clear opening/promise/primary CTA; links reach intended destinations.
- No horizontal overflow, important-content clipping, sticky collisions, broken headings, or poor crops.
- Exercise each present new/changed interaction to its visible result: menus, forms, tabs, calculators, galleries, configuration, links, CTAs.
- Keyboard, focus, labels, logical order; check contrast of actual pairs, not only palette documentation.
- Reduced motion, no hover dependency, missing media/error states where relevant.
- Inspect runtime errors/failed requests when exposed; assess scroll fluidity and loading shifts. Do not guess Core Web Vitals.
- Run build and existing required checks. Test nontrivial calculations/form/transformation logic when needed, not tests mirroring markup.

**Art-direction quality:**

- Does the selected material lead both opening and later compositions?
- Do scenes develop one idea with perceptible changes in scale, rhythm, or density?
- Do graphics/copy express this offer? If changing the logo makes it fit any company, strengthen the concept.
- Is a key later section as authored as the hero?
- Inspect details separately across scenes: does each caption contribute, do placement/scale/repetition of lines/symbols help, and did shared components impose the same decorative wrapper everywhere? Compare questionable cases without the detail or with another treatment on desktop/mobile; record material corrections. Quality is neither decoration count nor its absence.
- Does mobile preserve the idea instead of shrinking desktop?
- Does the memorable moment explain the offer and fit the whole, or does the static composition fulfill that role?
- For redesigns compare matching before/after viewports within promised scope. Correct code does not prove a new art direction.

**Palette and experience over time:**

- View each key sequence's entry, development, exit, and reverse scroll/selection. Try normal/faster scrolling for jumps, stuck sticky states, and delayed reactions.
- Verify actual plane/parallax relationships, masks, transforms, and motif handoff where promised. An `animation` declaration or library is not evidence.
- Capture corresponding frames across states or a recording where supported. A hero screenshot does not verify timing/transitions. Mark this part unverified if tools cannot observe it.
- Check mobile/reduced motion preserve meaning, access, and palette while reducing cost/energy.
- Compare surfaces, text, CTA, images, and effects with tokens/allowed tones. Inspect generated assets/blending for foreign colors, unused tokens, and arbitrary styles. Repair drift even when contrast passes.
- Update the matrix with concrete results/evidence. Missing principal sequences, palette compliance, or composition is failure even when build/click checks pass.

Record actual checks/findings, not a checklist labeled "passed". Fix material weaknesses and repeat affected checks. Deliver when criteria are met; do not design indefinitely.
